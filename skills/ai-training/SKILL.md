# AI Training Skill

Brand: **GRUMMAN AI**. Local-first preferred. Sourced claims only. No GH Actions.

## Principles
1. Prefer local/on-prem training (DGX Spark/GB10, Jetson clusters, local vLLM/Ollama). Document evidence for performance.
2. Source all performance claims, dataset sizes, model versions. Version datasets and models with provenance.
3. Integrate with Earth-2 / Weather-Forecasting for climate models, Isaac for simulation data.
4. Capture pipeline outputs or dashboard screenshots for READMEs.
